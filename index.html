🏸 Beginner's Guide to Badminton




What is Badminton?

Badminton is a fast and accessible racket sport. It's perfect for fitness, enjoyment, and friendly competition. It combines quick reactions, agility, coordination, and tactics.

📒 Badminton Rules

  • Badminton can be played singles or doubles.
  • Best of 3 games, first to 21 points
  • Shuttle must land within court
  • Serve diagonally behind the service line
  • Serve must be 1.15m or below
  • Each game, switch sides; at 11 points in the third game
  • Doubles players rotate around each other depending on the situation

🥅 Basic Equipment

  • Badminton birdie (shuttlecock)
    • Feather
    • Nylon/plastic
  • Racket
  • Net
  • Court

🎯 Tips and Tricks

  • Always practice.
    • Practicing your basic shots and footwork once or twice a week can help you improve quickly.
  • Learn from the pros.
    • By analyzing experts, you can recognize their techniques.
  • Get critiqued.
    • A coach can help you maximize your capabilities and teach you additional tips.
  • Relax your movements.
    • Learn to move at the correct timing.
    • Be light; move quickly.
