🏸 Beginner's Guide to Badminton



📒 Badminton Rules

  • Badminton can be played singles or doubles.
  • Best of 3 games, first to 21 points
  • Shuttle must land within court
  • Serve diagonally behind the service line
  • Serve must be 1.15m or below
  • Each game, switch sides; at 11 points in the third game
  • Doubles players rotate around each other depending on the situation

🥅 Basic Equipment

  • Badminton birdie (shuttlecock)
    • Feather
    • Nylon/plastic
  • Racket
  • Net
  • Court
